The challenge

30 locations, daily requests, one buried team.

SpeedFit was expanding fast across Australia and New Zealand, and the volume outgrew what any internal team could hold: social content, local area marketing, and promotional campaigns multiplying across every franchise location, with brand consistency on the line at each one.

The solution

A managed design team that runs itself.

Andelo became SpeedFit's production design team: one point of contact managing the queue, and the same designers on the account throughout, so the brand was learned once and applied the same way to every location's assets. That's how consistency held across 30 sites without SpeedFit policing it. They briefed, approved, and moved on.

The result

Three years of consistent output.

Across a three-year partnership, SpeedFit scaled hours up during campaign peaks and stabilised through quieter periods. No recruitment, no onboarding gaps, no brand drift across 30 locations, and franchisees at every site happy with the quality and speed. Consistent output, month after month, for three years.
