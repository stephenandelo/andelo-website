The Challenge

MaskCo moves fast. Their design output can't slow down.

Rapid product launches, always-on social, and high-volume campaigns across multiple channels. Their marketing calendar doesn't pause for EOFY, Black Friday, Christmas, or new product drops. Neither can their design output.

The Solution

Briefs in, polished work out.

Andelo runs as MaskCo's production design team every week of the year: campaign key visuals, daily social content, promotional email design, and website graphics. The MaskCo team briefs the work in and we deliver polished, on-brand design ready to use. That year-round rhythm is exactly what holds when the busy season hits. The team already knows the brand, so there's no scrambling for extra vendors and no onboarding freelancers mid-campaign. The volume goes up; the deadlines still get met.

The Result

Reliable in week one. Reliable in year two.

Two years of high-volume delivery, through every Black Friday, Christmas, and product launch, without a missed deadline. One consistent brand across paid ads, social, email, and web. The MaskCo team focuses on strategy; we handle the volume. No bottlenecks, no micromanagement.
