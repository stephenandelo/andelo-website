Common questions?

Every brief gets a committed delivery date before work starts, and we hit the date we give. Most work lands in 2-3 business days; small tasks in by 12pm Sydney can land same day. Bigger projects like multi-page decks or campaign packs get their date confirmed upfront, so you always know when it's landing.

Everything needed to deliver: design, revisions, and the coordination and quality checks that keep it all running. One number covers the whole job. Your safeguard is the estimate: every brief gets one before work starts, it acts as the ceiling, and if we come in under, only the actual time is deducted. You always know the maximum before we begin.

No caps on revision rounds. Revision time is tracked within your hours, and each round gets its own committed date. Our internal quality checks mean most work lands in 1-2 rounds, so your hours go to new work, not fixes. Estimates assume up to three rounds; if something needs more, we flag it before continuing, never after. Feedback works best as one consolidated set per round, and your Account Manager helps gather it.

Month-to-month. Cancel anytime: your plan runs to the end of the current billing period, then stops. No notice period, no fees. You can pause by whole months from your next renewal, and one email restarts everything within 3-5 business days. And you're covered by our money-back guarantee: a full first month on Pilot, or 14 days on Essentials and Premium.
