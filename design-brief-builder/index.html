Common questions

      

        

          
Is my brief sent anywhere?

          
No. Nothing you type leaves this page. No account, no email, no server. It runs entirely in your browser.

        

        

          
Why is this free?

          
Better briefs make design work better for everyone, including us. And a good brief is only half of it. The other half is an experienced designer turning it into great work. So the knowledge is free, and the expertise is what we sell.

        

        

          
Does this only work with Andelo?

          
No. It works for every production design type (graphics, presentations, print, digital). For web development, motion, or app design, use it as the base and add that discipline's specs.

        

        

          
Do small tasks really need a brief?

          
A repeat task with a designer who already knows your brand can skip the brief. The ones that need it are the firsts: a new designer, or a task that looks small but you've never briefed before. Don't assume the familiar request is the same request.

        

        

          
What if I can't answer every question?

          
Fill what you know and say what's still open. A brief that names its gaps (“copy is still in review”) beats one that leaves them empty: the designer knows it's incomplete, instead of assuming it's complete and building on the missing piece.
