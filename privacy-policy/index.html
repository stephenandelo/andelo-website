Your privacy matters to us. It is Andelo's policy to respect your privacy and to comply with any applicable law and regulation regarding personal information we may collect about you, including across our website, andelo.com.au, and any other sites we own and operate.

We are committed to handling personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

This policy is effective as of 6 May 2021 and was last updated on 26 September 2026.

Information we collect

We collect information you actively provide when using our services, and information sent automatically by your device when you access our site.

Log data. When you visit our website, our servers may automatically log standard data provided by your browser: your device's IP address, browser type and version, the pages you visit, the date and time of your visit, time spent on each page, and technical details of any errors you encounter. On its own this may not identify you, but combined with other data it may become identifying.

Personal information. We may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Company name and role
  • Social media profile details, where you interact with us on those platforms
  • Anything you choose to tell us in a message or another form on this site

Book a call enquiry form. If you submit the form on our Book a call page, we collect your name, work email, company, job title, your answers to a few questions about your situation and timing, anything you add in the optional message field, and an optional phone number if you choose to provide one. We use this information to reply to your enquiry and to suggest times for a call. Andelo stores it securely on our systems.

We do not collect payment card details through this website. Client billing is handled separately through our invoicing and payment providers.

Our free tools collect nothing

Our free tools, including the Task Brief Tool and the Capacity Calculator, run entirely in your browser. What you type into them is not transmitted to us or to any server, is not stored, and is not visible to us. There is no account, no email requirement, and no submission step. Standard log data described above still applies to the page visit itself.

Our free guides and articles are open pages. Reading them requires no email address and no sign-up.

How we collect it

We may collect personal information when you:

  • Submit the enquiry form on our Book a call page
  • Contact us by email, through another form, or via social media
  • Interact with us on social media, including mentions
  • Use a browser or device to access our content

Why we collect it

We only collect and use personal information where we have a legitimate reason, and only what is reasonably necessary to provide our services. We use it to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and arrange a call when you contact us through the Book a call form or otherwise
  • Provide and administer our services to clients
  • Improve how our website and tools work
  • Meet our legal, accounting, and record-keeping obligations

We do not sell your personal information. We do not send marketing emails to people who have not asked to hear from us.

Security

While we hold personal information, we protect it within commercially acceptable means against loss, theft, unauthorised access, disclosure, copying, use, or modification.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed. In the event of a data breach likely to result in serious harm, we will notify affected individuals and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner as required by the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purposes described in this policy. When it is no longer required, we delete it or de-identify it, unless we are required to retain it to meet a legal, accounting, or reporting obligation.

Disclosure to third parties

We may disclose personal information to:

  • Our team members and contractors, where they need it to deliver our services
  • Third-party service providers who support our operations, including IT, hosting, data storage, email, and analytics providers
  • Professional advisers such as our accountants and lawyers
  • Courts, tribunals, regulatory authorities, and law enforcement, where required by law or to establish, exercise, or defend legal rights

Our production team works across Australia, Indonesia, and the Philippines. Client materials sometimes contain personal information about third parties, for example people appearing in photography, testimonials, or case studies supplied to us. We handle that information only on our client's instructions, only for the purpose of delivering the commissioned work, and under confidentiality obligations. Responsibility for having the right consents to use those materials rests with the client who supplies them.

International transfers

Some of our service providers and team members are located outside Australia, including in the United States, Indonesia, and the Philippines. Where we transfer personal information overseas, we do so in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles and take reasonable steps to ensure it is handled consistently with this policy. Please note that overseas recipients may be subject to different data protection laws than those in Australia.

Your rights

You can choose not to give us personal information, though it may affect what we can do for you. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

You may request details of the personal information we hold about you. If you believe anything we hold is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, irrelevant, or misleading, contact us and we will take reasonable steps to correct it.

If you have agreed to receive communications from us and change your mind, you can opt out at any time. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

If you believe we have breached a data protection law, contact us first with the details. We will investigate promptly and respond in writing with the outcome and any steps we will take. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can escalate your complaint to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Cookies

We use cookies, small data files stored by your browser, to understand how our site is used and to improve it. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings; some parts of the site may not work as intended if you disable them.

External links

Our website may link to sites we do not operate. We have no control over their content or privacy practices and cannot accept responsibility for them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our processes, practices, or legal obligations. Any changes will be posted at this same link. Where the law requires it, we will seek your consent or give you the opportunity to opt in or out of new uses of your personal information.
